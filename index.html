Public Lands Accessibility & Restroom Upgrades

Please Note: Not all recreation sites, trailheads, and rustic areas on public lands currently feature accessible restrooms or ADA-compliant facilities. Under official guidelines from agencies like the U.S. Forest Service (USFS) and the Bureau of Land Management (BLM), older non-accessible vault toilets and facilities are systematically upgraded to modern accessible standards as existing infrastructure reaches the end of its service lifecycle and is scheduled for capital replacement.

How Your Feedback Helps: Community-reported submissions and real-world accessibility notes help identify where barrier-free access is most urgently needed. We aggregate this public feedback to work collaboratively with land managers, agencies, and advocacy groups to help prioritize accessibility improvements across our public trails and recreation areas.
